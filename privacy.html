Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains how VTuber Studio, a private desktop application, handles data. VTuber Studio is operated by its developer ("I", contact below) and is used exclusively to manage the YouTube channel Patchi (@patchi.v09). It is not offered to other users.

YouTube API Services

VTuber Studio uses YouTube API Services. By interacting with the channel's content you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service. Data processed by Google is subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

What data is accessed

  • Metadata and status of the channel's own videos (title, description, privacy status, publish time).
  • Aggregated YouTube Analytics for the channel's own videos (e.g. views, engaged views, average view duration, likes, comments, shares, subscribers gained).
  • Public comments posted on the channel's own videos: comment text, video ID and timestamp.

Commenters

If you comment on one of Patchi's videos, the comment text may be read by VTuber Studio and stored locally on my computer so it can be considered as a topic for a future video. Your name, profile picture and channel ID are not stored. A comment may be quoted as text in a later video only after manual review and without your name. Comment texts that are not used are deleted after 30 days.

Storage, sharing and retention

  • All data is stored locally on my own computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • Data obtained through the YouTube API is refreshed or deleted at least every 30 days. If a video is deleted from YouTube, its locally stored data is deleted at the next daily synchronisation.
  • Authorization tokens are stored only locally and never published.

Your rights and contact

You can ask for deletion of a stored comment text or ask questions about this policy at any time: niclaslieschke@gmail.com. The channel owner can revoke VTuber Studio's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Where the EU General Data Protection Regulation applies, the legal basis for processing comment texts is my legitimate interest in interacting with my audience (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

This website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. These providers may process your IP address to deliver the page; see the GitHub Privacy Statement and the Google Privacy Policy. This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics.
